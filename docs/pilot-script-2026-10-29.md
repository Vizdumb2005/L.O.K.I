# LOKI performer pilot: runner script and scorecard

Status: draft for use after a passing build. Human outcomes untested. This prepares a pilot; it does not authorize recruitment. First resolve the consent, data-handling and review prerequisites in `docs/pilot-protocol.md`.

## 1. Consent and setup

Read: "This is an on-screen mentalism performance, not mind reading or a test of you. We want to learn how it feels. It can be wrong. You may stop, skip or request deletion of your notes. We will record your answers and ratings under a participant code, not your name. Is that alright?"

Record consent, code, build/commit, effect order and device. Do not record before consent. Run only the two show effects; do not open the separate camera lab. Use the app's consent screen too.

## 2. Run each effect

The Twelve: "Choose one of the twelve displayed cards and keep it in mind. Do not say it, click it or tell me." Use the displayed set, not a physical deck.

Ember Words: "Choose one of the four displayed words and keep it to yourself." Do not extend the list.

Before each run, the participant privately writes the choice on a folded slip and keeps it face down. The runner must not see it until after ratings. If writing is declined, record post-run pointing/self-report as a different ground-truth method, not a prerecord.

For each: follow the app's prompts without adding hints, paraphrasing, answering for the participant or inserting extra performer lines. Let its cues finish or let the participant use Skip/Continue. Accept its reveal or honest miss. Stop immediately on request or discomfort.

During the run, note prompts, completion, reveal/miss, skips, interruptions and technical failures. Do not inspect or transcribe the private slip or discuss correctness yet. Do not praise a hit or rescue a miss.

## 3. Experience first, after each effect

Ask before discussing correctness or explaining the mechanism. For each item use 1 = not at all, 5 = very much; ask "What moment led to that rating?"

- Responsiveness: Did the next line feel responsive to your last answer?
- Felt liveness: Did this feel like a personal, one-to-one interaction?
- Suspense: How much suspense did you feel?
- Freedom: How free did you feel to choose?
- Enjoyment: How much did you enjoy it?
- Clarity: How clear were the instructions?

Then ask: "How do you think it worked?" Record the explanation verbatim where possible; code separately as explains method / notices cue only / unsure. Do not coach.

## 4. Correctness and debrief

Only now turn over the slip and record actual choice, revealed choice or miss, and correct / wrong / not attempted. Record any non-prerecorded alternative separately. A related word is wrong, not a rescued hit.

Read: "The app uses your selected answers and a confidence gate. It does not read minds, use the camera in the show or detect lies. A miss is not your failure." Explain on request, including immediately if requested earlier; record that timing deviation. Offer deletion.

## Scorecard: one row per effect

Code | build | effect/order | started | completed | reveal/miss | actual/revealed | ground-truth method | correct/wrong/NA | dropout | technical failure | six separate ratings | method explanation/code | skips/deviations | notes/deletion

Keep abstention, dropout, failure and correctness separate. Never combine ratings into an "accuracy" score.

Provenance: first draft by the LOKI junior teammate (Gmail collaborator), revised and corrected by the lead. Neither version is a validated study instrument. Ratings are asked before the private slip is opened; correctness is disclosed only after ratings.
